Tools from the firm

Numbers worth running.

Quiet, well-built tools for thinking through the math behind the longer-horizon decisions in your plan.

I Calculator

Human Life Value

A simple tool for calculating the present economic value of your future earnings. The result gives you a defensible number for how much income your family depends on you to produce over the coming decades.

  • The true amount of protection those who depend on you would need.
  • Calculated from the greater of two figures: your age and income, or your age and net worth.
  • A clear number our team can help you put in place, based on your age and health.

II Calculator

Retirement Savings

A projection tool for modeling your retirement savings trajectory. Adjust the inputs to see whether you are tracking toward the life you have designed for retirement, or whether the math suggests an adjustment is worth making now.

  • Whether your current savings rate keeps you on track to retire comfortably.
  • Built on the few inputs that truly drive the result, with none of the usual filler.
  • On track or off, our team can show you how to close the gap or retire sooner.
