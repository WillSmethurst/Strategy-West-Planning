About Strategy West Planning

There is no template for the life you are building. The work begins with that fact.

Strategy West Planning is a financial planning firm built around the families it serves, not around the products it sells. The work begins with learning who you are, and it continues with telling you the truth.

Why we built this firm

The strategies that have quietly built family legacy for decades are also the most misunderstood.

For decades, certain financial strategies have quietly produced legacy for families who understood how to use them. The same strategies have been mis-sold, oversold, and broadly misunderstood by far more people than they ever should have been.

We built Strategy West Planning to help families see the difference. Not to defend any particular product. Not to sell you on the latest pitch. To help you actually understand what is available, what fits your family, and what the structure you build will do across the decades it has to hold.

The work is the education. The strategy follows from that.

How we approach the work

No two families want the same legacy, and no two families need the same structure.

The work begins with learning who you are, what you are trying to build, and what you want to leave behind. From there, we tailor a strategy to those goals, not to a template, not to a quota, not to whatever happens to be in fashion that year.

If what you need is something we do not offer, we will say so. Our reputation is built on telling families the truth, even when the truth means recommending something else. We would rather you walk away with the right plan than stay with the wrong one.

The firms that endure are the ones their clients trust. We built this one to be that.

Begin the Conversation

A first call answers one question. Whether we should keep talking.

The initial meeting is short, exploratory, and honest. We will ask about your family, your goals and ambitions, and what you are trying to build, professionally and personally. For business owners, the conversation extends to the business itself. If we are not the right fit, we will tell you. If we are, the work continues into deeper strategy sessions with our team, planning around the topics that help you, your family, and your business take the next true step toward the life you are building.
